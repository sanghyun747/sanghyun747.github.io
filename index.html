About

I am an undergraduate student in Biomedical Engineering at Yonsei University and an undergraduate researcher in Prof. Ki-Jae Hong’s lab, where I work on actigraphy-based biosignal analysis and classification of depression-related labels.

My interest in research began through the natural sciences track in high school, where I studied life sciences and completed an early independent research project.

Alongside my lab work, I build evidence-grounded AI systems and rapid prototypes that connect data pipelines, backend services, and user-facing applications.

Selected Projects

Jul 2026 AI Agent / Hackathon

ICML Review Agent — Ralphthon @ICML

I built an evidence-grounded review agent that mapped paper claims to exact PDF passages, checked review consistency, and generated structured ICML-style reviews through a deadline-aware submission pipeline. During the live event, the system successfully processed and submitted nine validated review assignments.

A post-event comparison evaluated evidence traceability, portability, and deployment reliability against the public reference implementation.

Role: Developer · Team: Hyungbin Park, Sanghyun Park, Minjae Jin

Private repository · architecture summary available on request

2026 Research Prototype

ActiScope Depression Research Console

A research prototype for actigraphy-based classification of depression-related labels, built in Prof. Ki-Jae Hong’s lab. It combines participant time-series views, group comparison, preprocessing, class-imbalance handling, model evaluation, and CSV-based prediction workflows in one console.

Role: Sole developer, undergraduate research project

Private repository · architecture summary available on request

2026 MVP / Hackathon

Olive Better MVP — Cofathon (KRAFTON × Olive Young)

A wellness merchandising prototype in which an operator enters a planning request and the system gathers public search evidence, filters products against safety rules, and drafts banner copy and a customer view for review before publishing. Customers see only published drafts, and their reactions feed back into product scoring for the next draft.

Role: Developer · Team: Sanghyun Park, Jihyo Park, Hyunkyu Lee, Minjae Jin

Submission report Source & screenshots Download (zip) Runs locally with Node.js; no hosted demo.

2026–Present

Undergraduate Researcher, Ki-Jae Hong Lab

Department of Biomedical Engineering, Yonsei University. I build the research pipeline for actigraphy-based classification of depression-related labels, using data inspired by the HCHS/SOL dataset: 30-second activity records, fixed-length time-series tensors, Z-score normalization, class-imbalance handling, a Random Forest and XGBoost comparison, threshold tuning, and ROC/AUC evaluation. The pipeline is packaged as the ActiScope research console.
